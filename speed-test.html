Spacebar CPS Test: How Fast Can You Click?

Welcome to our free Spacebar CPS Test (also called Spacebar Click Test or Spacebar Speed Test). This tool tests how many times you can press the spacebar key on your keyboard in one second. Whether you play Minecraft, Roblox, shooter games, or you want to see if your fingers are fast enough for Spacebar Clicker, this counter measures your exact clicking speed.

How to Click the Spacebar Faster (Techniques that Work)

If your score is stuck around 5 or 6 CPS and you want to jump to 9 or 10 CPS, here are simple methods you can practice right now:

  • 1. Butterfly Clicking (Two-Finger Method): Don't use only your thumb! Put your right index finger and right middle finger over the spacebar. Tap them one after another like two drum sticks. Because two fingers share the work, your CPS can almost double.
  • 2. Two-Hand Spacebar Method: Place your left thumb on the left side of the spacebar and your right thumb on the right side. Alternate tapping left, right, left, right. This reduces finger tiredness in longer 30s or 60s modes.
  • 3. Don't Smash Too Hard: Pushing the spacebar key all the way down into the plastic desk takes extra milliseconds for the key to bounce back up. Try pressing just enough to actuate the switch, then release quickly.
  • 4. Use a Mechanical Keyboard: Laptop keyboards and cheap rubber dome keyboards have soft spongy feel and slow rebound. A mechanical keyboard with red or silver switches springs back up immediately, letting you register faster repeat clicks.
  • 5. Warm Up Your Fingers: If your hands are cold, your muscles move stiffly. Shake your hands and warm up with a quick 5-second test before trying your record.

Spacebar Click Test FAQ

What is a good spacebar clicking speed?

An average person clicks between 5 to 7 CPS on the spacebar. If you reach 8 CPS or higher, you are already faster than 80% of computer users.

Can I hold down the spacebar to get infinite score?

No, we programmed this test to block automatic OS key-repeat. If you hold down the spacebar, it only counts as 1 click. You must physically lift your finger and press again each time.

Does spacebar test work on phone and tablet?

Yes! On mobile or iPad, you can tap the big on-screen spacebar button. You can even tap with two thumbs at the same time to see how fast your mobile touch speed is.

How does this test help with Spacebar Clicker game?

In Spacebar Clicker, you can buy Keyboard Upgrades that double each click. If you click 10 CPS with 4 Keyboard Upgrades, you generate hundreds of points per second manually! Read our Spacebar Clicker Upgrades Guide to see how to use your speed in the game.
