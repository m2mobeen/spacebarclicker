What is a Spacebar CPS Test?

A Spacebar CPS Test (Clicks Per Second test) measures how many times you can press the spacebar within a set time. Whether you're a competitive gamer optimizing your APM (Actions Per Minute), a student testing your keyboard's responsiveness, or just curious about your personal record, this tool gives you instant, accurate results.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Choose a duration — 1 second for a pure burst test, up to 60 seconds for endurance.
  2. Press SPACEBAR (or tap the button on mobile) to start the timer and register your first click.
  3. Keep clicking as fast as you can until the timer runs out.
  4. See your score in CPS (clicks per second) and total clicks.

Tips to Improve Your Spacebar Clicking Speed

  • Butterfly Technique: Alternate between two fingers on the spacebar to effectively double your rate.
  • Jitter Clicking: Tense your arm to create controlled vibrations that transfer to rapid clicks. Advanced but highly effective.
  • Relax Your Wrist: Tension is the enemy of speed. Keep your wrist loose and let your fingers do the work.
  • Use a Mechanical Keyboard: Switches with shorter actuation distances (like Cherry MX Speed Silver) allow faster repeat presses.
  • Practice Consistently: Like any motor skill, spacebar speed improves with deliberate daily practice.
