Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

At Spacebar Clicker (spacebarclicker.win), your privacy is a priority. This policy explains what information we handle and how.

Data We Collect

We collect no personally identifiable information. Spacebar Clicker is a client-side browser game. There is no login, no account, and no form that transmits your data to our servers during normal gameplay.

Local Storage (Game Save)

Your game progress — total score, purchased upgrades, and upgrade levels — is saved using your browser's localStorage. This data:

  • Stays entirely on your own device.
  • Is never transmitted to any server.
  • Can be cleared at any time using the "reset game" link in the game footer, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Server Log Files

Like all websites, our hosting provider automatically collects standard server log data when you visit: IP address, browser type, referring page, and timestamp. This is standard hosting infrastructure data, not linked to any personal identity, and is retained for a maximum of 30 days.

Cookies

Spacebar Clicker does not set any tracking or advertising cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any equivalent tracking tool.

Third-Party Links

Our pages contain links to external sites (GitHub, CrazyGames, YouTube, IndieDB). When you click these links, you leave our site and are subject to those sites' own privacy policies. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for their content or practices.

Children's Privacy

Spacebar Clicker does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through our site, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site constitutes acceptance of any updates.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use our Contact page.
