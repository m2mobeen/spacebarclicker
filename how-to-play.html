1. What is Spacebar Clicker?

Spacebar Clicker is a browser-based idle/incremental game built around the iconic key at the center of your keyboard. The core loop is simple: press spacebar → earn points → buy automation → watch numbers grow exponentially.

The game runs entirely in your browser with zero dependencies. Your progress is saved locally via localStorage and persists across sessions without an account.

2. Your First Click

When you load Spacebar Clicker, press the spacebar or click the on-screen button. Each press earns 1 point by default. Your first goal is 30 points to buy your first Monkey — once purchased, passive income begins.

  • Desktop: Physical spacebar key or left-click the button.
  • Mobile: Tap the on-screen SPACEBAR button.
  • Both inputs can be used simultaneously.

3. Core Mechanics

The game tracks two key values:

  • Total Points: Your currency — spent on upgrades, earned by clicking and automation.
  • Points Per Second (PPS): Passive income from purchased helpers.

As your PPS increases, small spacebar icons rain down the screen — a visual indicator of your empire's scale.

4. Upgrades & ROI

There are 10 upgrade tiers. Nine increase your PPS (passive helpers), and one — Keyboard Upgrade — multiplies your manual click value. See full stats on the Upgrades page.

💡 Keyboard Upgrade Stack

Keyboard Upgrades stack multiplicatively. 3 upgrades = 2³ = 8× per click (not 6×). Prioritize these if you are an active player.

5. Early, Mid & Late Game Strategy

Early Game (0 – 10,000 pts)

Buy Monkeys immediately. Transition to Boomer Moms as soon as possible — their PPS/cost ratio is superior. Do not save for Gen Z Kids too early; multiple Boomer Moms outperform one Gen Z Kid in total output initially.

Mid Game (10,000 – 1,000,000 pts)

Shift to Angry Influencers and MOBA Gamers. Passive income should now far exceed manual clicking. Add Keyboard Upgrades when their cost represents less than 5 seconds of your current PPS.

Late Game (1,000,000+ pts)

Focus on Nuclear Blast Guns and Alien Tech Machines. ROI calculations matter most here — check whether an upgrade pays for itself within a minute of PPS output before purchasing.

6. Advanced Tips

  1. Dual Input: Mouse-click the button AND press spacebar simultaneously — both register as separate clicks.
  2. Keep Tab Active: Browsers throttle JavaScript in background tabs. Keep the game in a visible window.
  3. Speed Test First: Visit the Spacebar Speed Test to benchmark your clicking rate.
  4. Don't Oversave: If an upgrade costs more than ~500× your PPS, buy a cheaper tier instead.

7. FAQ

Does the game save automatically?

Yes — every 100ms to localStorage. Clearing browser data or using incognito mode will erase saves.

Is there a maximum score?

No hard cap. The display supports septillions (10²⁴). With max Alien Tech, reaching trillions per second is achievable within hours.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes. The game is fully responsive. Tap the on-screen spacebar — all upgrades and mechanics work identically.
