Why is Spacebar CPS Slower Than Mouse CPS?

Many gamers reach 9 or 10 CPS on a computer mouse, but when they try spacebar on keyboard, they only get 6 CPS. Why does this happen?

  • The Spacebar is the Heaviest Key: Unlike normal alphabet keys or mouse buttons, the spacebar is long and has a metal wire stabilizer underneath. It takes more physical force to push down.
  • Longer Key Travel: A standard computer keyboard key travels about 3.5 to 4 millimeters down before it registers. A gaming mouse click button only moves about 0.5 to 1 millimeter! More travel distance means each click takes longer.
  • Spring Rebound Delay: The spacebar takes a few extra milliseconds to bounce back to the top position. If you press again before the key springs back up, the click does not register.

What is Average Spacebar CPS by Test Duration?

Your CPS changes a lot depending on how long you have to click:

  • 1 Second Burst: 8.0 to 12.0 CPS. In only one second, you don't get tired, so people usually score their highest CPS here.
  • 5 Seconds (Standard): 6.0 to 9.0 CPS. The most balanced test mode. Good mix of burst speed and rhythm.
  • 10 Seconds: 5.5 to 8.0 CPS. Finger tiredness starts to kick in around second 7.
  • 30 to 60 Seconds (Endurance): 4.5 to 7.0 CPS. In marathon mode, maintaining high speed without stopping is very hard on your wrist.
