Spacebar Clicker Upgrades Guide: Best Order & Last Upgrade

Welcome to our complete upgrades guide for Spacebar Clicker. When you play this game, you see many upgrades on the right side, but the game does not give you big manual. You have to spend your hard clicked points and wonder if this upgrade is waste of score or really good. Here we answer the most asked questions first, give you the full upgrade list table with costs, and show you the best upgrade order strategy.

What is the Best Upgrade in Spacebar Clicker?

The best upgrade for early passive points is Boomer Mom, but Keyboard Upgrade is the most powerful upgrade in the whole game if you like clicking the spacebar with your own hands.

Here is why: Boomer Mom costs only 120 points and gives 3 points every second. Compare this to Monkey which costs 30 points for only 0.2 points every second. One Boomer Mom is worth 15 Monkeys! So for early game idle score, Boomer Mom gives you the best return for your points.

However, when you reach mid game, Keyboard Upgrade becomes totally broken. Every Keyboard Upgrade you buy doubles your manual spacebar press. If you click 8 times per second on our Spacebar Click Test and you have 4 Keyboard Upgrades, every tap gives 16 points. That means you get more than 120 points per second just from tapping spacebar! So the best upgrade depends if you want to leave tab open (Boomer Mom and Robots) or smash spacebar yourself (Keyboard Upgrade).

What is the Last Upgrade in Spacebar Clicker?

The last upgrade in Spacebar Clicker is the Alien Tech Key Pressing Machine, which costs a base price of 80,000,000 points (80 Million) and gives 1,000,000 clicks every second.

There is no tier 11 upgrade after Alien Tech. Once you buy Alien Tech Key Pressing Machine, you reached the final tier of the game. Every next Alien Tech you buy gets 40% more expensive (×1.40 cost multiplier), but each one also gets faster. When you have 3 or 4 Alien Tech machines running, your points-per-second reaches tens of millions, and spacebars rain like crazy storm on your screen.

Spacebar Clicker Talent Guide: Active vs Idle Build

A lot of players search on Google for "space clicker talent guide" or look for talent tree button. In Spacebar Clicker, there is no separate RPG talent tree menu with skill points. Instead, your "talents" are the two different playstyles you choose when buying upgrades:

  • Active Clicker Talent Build: You put your points into Keyboard Upgrades first. You also buy helpers, but your main point generator is your own fingers pressing spacebar. If you have mechanical keyboard or good butterfly click technique, this talent build gives you massive bursts of points to unlock higher tiers fast.
  • Idle / AFK Talent Build: You ignore expensive Keyboard Upgrades and only buy auto helpers (Monkeys, Boomer Moms, Gen Z Kids, Robots, Laser Guns). You leave the browser tab open while working, studying, or watching video, and collect points automatically without touching the keyboard.
  • Hybrid Build (Recommended): You let passive helpers run while you are away, and whenever you sit at keyboard, you buy 1 or 2 Keyboard Upgrades and click fast to jump over the expensive price hurdles.

Suggest Effective Upgrade Strategies to Maximize Scores

If you want to reach millions of points without wasting time, follow this exact upgrade step order:

Step 1: Early Game (0 to 1,000 points)

Start clicking spacebar until you reach 30 points. Buy 1 Monkey right away so points never stop ticking. Keep tapping spacebar until you have 3 to 5 Monkeys. Then stop buying Monkeys! Save your points directly for Boomer Mom (120 points). Buy 3 to 5 Boomer Moms. Once you earn 20 points per second, save for Gen Z Kid (500 points).

Step 2: Mid Game (1,000 to 1,000,000 points)

Do not rush Keyboard Upgrade too early because 6,000 points is big price when you only earn 20 per second. First buy 3 Gen Z Kids and 2 Angry Influencers (10,000 points). Now your passive score gives over 400 per second. At this moment, buy your first Keyboard Upgrade (6,000 points). Now each time you press spacebar you get 2 points. If you click 100 times, you get 200 points easy. Next, buy MOBA Gamer and Homemade Robot to push your income past 5,000 points per second.

Step 3: Late Game (1,000,000 to 100,000,000+ points)

Notice that Laser Machine Gun (2,000,000) gives 25,000 per second. It is super cheap for how much it produces! Buy 3 or 4 Laser Guns before saving for Nuclear Blast Gun (10,000,000). Once you have Nuclear Gun, your score will hit 80,000,000 in few minutes. Buy Alien Tech Key Pressing Machine to beat the game.

Upgrade Cost Scaling: Why Things Get Expensive

Every time you buy one helper, the next one costs more points. Here is the exact cost multiplier math the game uses:

Upgrade Cost Increase Per Level Example: Level 1 vs Level 5 Cost
Monkey +10% (×1.10) Level 1: 30 pts → Level 5: 44 pts
Boomer Mom +30% (×1.30) Level 1: 120 pts → Level 5: 343 pts
Gen Z Kid +40% (×1.40) Level 1: 500 pts → Level 5: 1,920 pts
Keyboard Upgrade +250% (×3.50) Level 1: 6,000 pts → Level 3: 73,500 pts
Angry Influencer +50% (×1.50) Level 1: 10,000 pts → Level 4: 33,750 pts
MOBA Gamer & Above +40% (×1.40) Scales by 1.40 for each next purchase

Because Keyboard Upgrade costs 3.5 times more every purchase, don't buy it if it wipes out your entire bank. Only buy Keyboard Upgrade when your passive income can refill your points quickly.
