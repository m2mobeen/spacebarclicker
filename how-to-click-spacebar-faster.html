How to Click the Spacebar Faster: Best Techniques & Tips

If you want to know how to click the spacebar faster for gaming, typing speed, or to get billions of points in Spacebar Clicker, this guide gives you the 5 best techniques that actually work. You don't need fancy tools or software hacks — just simple finger habits you can start right now.

5 Techniques to Click the Spacebar Faster

Technique 1: Butterfly Clicking (Two Fingers Alternating)

This is the number one method used by competitive players. Instead of using your thumb, place your index finger and middle finger of your right hand together over the middle of the spacebar.

Tap index, then middle, then index, then middle. It should feel like walking your fingers on the desk. Because one finger goes down while the other finger is coming up, you cut the waiting time in half. With just 5 minutes of practice, most people go from 6 CPS to 9 or 10 CPS immediately.

Technique 2: Two-Hand Alternating Thumbs Method

If your fingers feel clumsy doing butterfly clicking, try the two-hand method:

  • Place your left thumb on the left side of the spacebar.
  • Place your right thumb on the right side of the spacebar.
  • Tap left thumb, right thumb, left thumb, right thumb in steady rhythm.

This method is super comfortable and is especially great for longer 30-second or 60-second speed tests because your hands don't get tired as fast.

Technique 3: Don't Bottom Out the Key (Feathering)

A huge mistake beginners make is smashing the spacebar as hard as possible down into the keyboard plate. Smashing hard does not make the computer register faster clicks! In fact, it does the opposite: pressing deep forces the switch to travel 4 millimeters down, and you have to wait for the spring to push all 4 millimeters back up.

Instead, practice feathering: press the spacebar just deep enough until the computer registers the click, then immediately release. Short, quick, light taps allow the key to bounce back twice as fast.

Technique 4: Mechanical Keyboard with Linear Switches

If you are playing on a cheap laptop keyboard or mushy office rubber dome keyboard, your hardware is holding you back. Rubber dome keyboards have a soft silicone cup underneath that takes time to pop back up.

Mechanical keyboards with Linear Red switches or Speed Silver switches have smooth movement with no bump and high rebound force. The spacebar key springs back up instantly, giving you an easy 1 to 2 extra CPS without any extra finger effort.

Technique 5: Relax Your Hand and Wrist

When people try to click fast, their instinct is to tense up their forearm and shoulder. But muscle tension causes your muscles to lock up after 3 or 4 seconds. Keep your wrist flat on the desk or mousepad, keep your shoulder relaxed, and let the movement come from your knuckles, not your whole arm.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you butterfly click on a normal spacebar?

Yes! Because the spacebar is the longest key on the keyboard, there is plenty of room to fit two fingers (index and middle) side by side. It is actually easier to butterfly click on a spacebar than on a small mouse button.

What is a good CPS score after practicing?

A score between 8.0 and 10.0 CPS is considered very good for keyboard spacebar. Anything above 11.0 CPS is elite pro gamer level. Read our Good Spacebar CPS Guide for full tier breakdowns.

Will clicking fast break my spacebar?

Modern keyboards can handle tens of millions of key presses. As long as you tap lightly and do not slam your fist down, fast clicking will not damage your keyboard.
